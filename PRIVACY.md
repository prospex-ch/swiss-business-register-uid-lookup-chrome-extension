# Privacy policy

*Swiss Business Register – UID Lookup by Prospex*, last updated 26 September 2026.

The extension does not collect, store or sell personal data, and it has no analytics.

- **Pages you visit**: the content script scans page text for Swiss UIDs (`CHE-123.456.789`) inside your browser. Page content is never sent anywhere.
- **Lookups**: when you hover a UID, use the right-click lookup or search in the popup, the UID or the text you selected or typed is sent to the public Zefix API of the Swiss Federal Office of Justice (`www.zefix.admin.ch`) to fetch the register entry. Zefix's own terms apply to that request.
- **Storage**: lookup results are cached in the browser's session storage for one hour and cleared when the browser closes. Your highlight setting is saved in Chrome sync storage.
- **Links**: "Full profile on Prospex" opens prospex.ch with UTM parameters that name the extension as the source. Nothing about you or the page you were on is added to the link.
